How we picked

We looked for packs that are free to download, are made for the PS1 / PSX look (low polygon counts, small textures, or tools for that look), and say clearly what you may do with them. Each entry that is not ours was opened on its own page, and the facts above are copied from that page on the date shown (our own three entries come from our repository data, as the end of this page says). A number the page does not give is not guessed: it is left out or shown as "not stated".

Every entry gets up to 5 points on four criteria and up to 4 on preview images, 24 in total, and the list is sorted by that total. The same rules and the same code score every entry, including the 3 that come from our own store.

  • Licence clarity (1 to 5). 5 = a standard named licence (CC0, CC BY, MIT...) is written on the page; 4 = a plain-language custom licence that covers commercial use and the main restriction; 3 = the page only says commercial use is fine; 2 = several licences listed or wording that does not settle the question; 1 = nothing readable.
  • Commercial use (0 to 5). 5 = allowed and no credit asked; 4 = allowed with credit; 3 = allowed with extra conditions; 1 = not stated; 0 = not allowed.
  • Content size (1 to 5). Counted from the page, with different steps per group. Models and 3D props: 1 to 5 = 1, 6 to 19 = 2, 20 to 49 = 3, 50 to 99 = 4, 100 or more = 5. Characters: 1 = 1, 2 to 4 = 2, 5 to 9 = 3, 10 to 14 = 4, 15 or more = 5. Textures (unique, not counting the same texture in several sizes): under 10 = 1, 10 to 39 = 2, 40 to 99 = 3, 100 to 299 = 4, 300 or more = 5. Shader files or effects: 1 = 1, 2 to 3 = 2, 4 to 6 = 3, 7 to 12 = 4, 13 or more = 5. Audio tracks or sounds and fonts or UI pieces: under 5 = 1, 5 to 14 = 2, 15 to 29 = 3, 30 to 99 = 4, 100 or more = 5. A count the page does not give scores 1.
  • Preview images (1 to 4). The number of preview images or screenshots shown on the page, counted, not judged by eye: none = 1, one = 2, two or three = 3, four or more = 4. It is capped at 4 because a long gallery is not better evidence, and galleries can include pictures of other products. It measures how much you can see before downloading, not how good the pictures are.
  • Maintenance (1 to 5). The newest date shown on the page (version date, update date, devlog entry, latest commit, or the posting date when it is the only one) compared with the check date: within 12 months = 5, within 24 = 4, within 36 = 3, within 60 = 2, older or no date shown = 1.

The total is out of 24. Ties are broken by licence clarity, then by the newest update date, then by title. Rank numbers run across the whole list, so a texture pack and a model pack can be compared; inside each group the entries appear in rank order. We list downloads that hold at least 4 usable items. Single-model uploads, smaller packs and profiles of separate single-model uploads such as a Sketchfab account are not listed (they are named below). Our eight sampler pages count as one entry, scored on the largest (8 models).

Pages read on 21 and 22 November 2026: itch.io, OpenGameArt, the Godot Asset Library, Sketchfab, and two GitHub repositories (LICENSE and README files and the date of the latest commit). The entries from our own store come from the Lowbit Lab repository data collected on 20 November 2026 and were not opened again; they are scored with the same rules and the same code as the others.

Scores compare listings on a small set of measurable facts. They are not a verdict on art quality: open the preview images and judge the style for yourself.
